• Empirical Justification: The 30 semantic features retain sufficient acoustic variance to achieve classification performance comparable to black-box models trained on all 167 raw features, while preserving 100% human interpretability.

SLIDE 4: FUZZY C-MEANS & 4 CRITERIA FOR REPRESENTATIVE X-AXIS SELECTION
========================================================================================
Title: Fuzzy C-Means Modeling & 4 Criteria for Representative Features
Fuzzy Clustering:
• Applied Fuzzy C-Means (FCM, fuzziness parameter m = 2.0) across each semantic axis to establish data-driven membership functions satisfying Ruspini partition of unity (Sum of mu_i = 1.0).
The 4 Selection Criteria for the Representative X-Axis Feature:
1. Direct Semantic Definition: The feature must directly define the physical and perceptual quantity of the axis (e.g., tempo for Tempo, pitch_mean for Pitch, energy_mean for Energy).
2. Monotonic Mean Progression: The representative feature MUST exhibit strictly increasing mean values across successive fuzzy levels (Level 1 < Level 2 < ... < Level k) to guarantee ordinal consistency.
3. Highest Variance Separation Ratio: The feature must achieve the highest between-cluster to within-cluster variance ratio (ANOVA F-statistic / Fisher criterion) among candidate features in that group.
4. Academic Extension for Multi-Dimensional Axes: For high-dimensional axes without a single scalar definition:
   • Timbre (13 MFCCs): mfcc_01_mean is selected as it captures the primary spectral envelope and dominant acoustic power.
   • Harmony (7 Chromas): Mean pitch-class energy is used to represent modal harmonic fullness.

SLIDE 5: GENERATION OF NEW CRISP & CONTINUOUS FUZZY DATASETS
========================================================================================
Title: Generating Dual Fuzzy Datasets from Membership Logic
Transformation Engine:
• The continuous acoustic samples are mapped into two distinct, structured fuzzy datasets:
1. Crisp Linguistic Dataset (features_fuzzy_crisp.csv):
   • 7 categorical attributes mapping each sample to its maximum membership set:
     x -> argmax_i mu_i(x) (e.g., Tempo = 'Moderate', Energy = 'Very Weak', Timbre = 'Low').
   • Shape: 3,600 samples x 9 columns (7 linguistic axes + sample_idx + label).
2. Continuous Fuzzy Membership Dataset (features_fuzzy_continuous.csv):
   • 27 continuous attributes in range [0, 1] preserving exact membership degrees across all fuzzy sets:
     [mu_Tempo_Slow, mu_Tempo_Mod, mu_Tempo_Fast, ..., mu_Spectral_High].
   • Shape: 3,600 samples x 29 columns (27 fuzzy membership degrees + sample_idx + label).
Key Takeaway: Eliminates artificial hard boundary splits and preserves micro-variation gradient information.

SLIDE 7: AUTOMATED SYMBOLIC RULE EXTRACTION FROM DT & RF
========================================================================================
Title: Automated Symbolic IF-THEN Rule Extraction
Extraction Methodology:
• Traversed all 50 constituent decision trees in the Random Forest ensemble.
• Converted all decision pathways from root to leaf into Disjunctive Normal Form (DNF) IF-THEN rules:
  IF (Condition 1) AND (Condition 2) ... AND (Condition k) THEN -> Genre [Confidence, Support, Frequency]
Pruning & Statistical Significance Thresholds:
• Minimum Confidence >= 50.0% (rule precision on training set).
• Minimum Support >= 10 samples (statistical validity).
• Tree Voting Frequency: Number of trees independently activating the identical rule (e.g., 3/50, 4/50 trees).
Selected Discovered Golden Rules:
• Ca trù: IF Energy == 'Very Weak' AND Vibrato IN ['Moderate', 'Heavy'] AND Timbre IN ['Very Low', 'Low'] THEN -> Ca trù (Conf: 75.0%, Supp: 260)
• Quan họ: IF Tempo == 'Moderate' AND Energy IN ['Strong', 'Very Strong'] AND Timbre IN ['High', 'Very High'] THEN -> Quan họ (Conf: 84.0%, Supp: 327)
• Cải lương: IF Vibrato == 'Heavy Ornament' AND Timbre IN ['High', 'Very High'] AND Harmony == 'High' THEN -> Cải lương (Conf: 78.4%, Supp: 218)
